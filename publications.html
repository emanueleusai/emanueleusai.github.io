Selected papers

Papers from the CMS experiment list every member of the collaboration as an author. These are the papers where I made a major contribution, newest first. The physics behind them is explained on the research page.

Reading the titles: pp means proton-proton, √s is the total energy of the two colliding protons, t is a top quark and t̄ (“t-bar”) its antiparticle, so tt̄ is a top quark-antiquark pair.

  1. 2023

    Evidence for four-top quark production in proton-proton collisions at √s = 13 TeV

    CMS Collaboration

    Phys. Lett. B 844 (2023) 138076

    Evidence that proton collisions at the LHC can produce four top quarks at once.

    doi:10.1016/j.physletb.2023.138076 arXiv:2303.03864
  2. 2022

    Four-top quark physics at the LHC

    F. Blekman, F. Déliot, V. Dutta and E. Usai

  3. 2022

    End-to-end jet classification of boosted top quarks with the CMS open data

    M. Andrews et al.

    Phys. Rev. D 105 (2022) 052008

    Deep learning that recognizes jets from high-momentum (“boosted”) top quarks directly from low-level detector data, using public CMS Open Data.

    doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.105.052008
  4. 2020

    Deep Learning the Morphology of Dark Matter Substructure

    S. Alexander et al.

    doi:10.1016/j.physletb.2015.02.048 arXiv:1409.5761
